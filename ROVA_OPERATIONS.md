# ROVA operations

The initial environment represents a controlled Accra pilot. The fictional operator's demo compliance checks are valid so the ride lifecycle can be exercised; newly evaluated compliance requirements fail closed unless every required item is `VALID`.

Operational controls for a real pilot must include configurable service-zone enablement, support escalation, document review queues, configurable notification intervals, staff role separation, incident management, and auditable activation decisions. The current operations portal is read-only for trip history and displays demo service availability.

Emergency/SOS, police/medical escalation, real identity review, and customer support are not operational services in this starter.
